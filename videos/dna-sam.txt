HOOK
It is a smart filing system for storing computer files inside DNA, so even huge collections can be found again.

WHAT IT IS
DNA-SaM: a DNA storage system tested in the lab and in living cells with 100% recovery.

WHAT IT DOES
DNA-SaM combines fast encoding, checks that avoid problem DNA sequences, and a two-level index for large files. Think of it like this: Like a library that labels every shelf and refuses to print pages that would jam the photocopier.

BEFORE
Most DNA-storage codecs are slow or create sequences that break lab steps.

AFTER
Who could benefit: DNA synthesis and sequencing toolmakers.
