FrontierRadar explainer: DuoPD: small compute units inside HBM4 memory could make AI chatbots answer up to 2.2× faster
Issue: 2026-10-30 | Stage 1 · Paper | Readiness 27/100 | Voice: Piper en_US-lessac-medium (local)

HOOK
Putting tiny processors inside AI memory could make chatbots answer 2.2 times faster.

WHAT IT IS
DuoPD adds small compute units to the base layer of HBM4 memory.

ANALOGY
Think of a warehouse that adds a packing desk on site, instead of trucking every item to a distant factory and back.

HOW IT WORKS
First, the GPU handles the prompt. Second, the memory handles word-by-word answering.

BEFORE
Before, every answer word meant moving data between memory and GPU.

AFTER
In simulation, throughput rises up to 2.23 times, and energy falls up to 37.9 percent.

WHY IT MATTERS
Simulation only, and memory vendors must buy in. SK hynix, Samsung and Micron are exposure. Our judgment: wait. Not financial advice.
