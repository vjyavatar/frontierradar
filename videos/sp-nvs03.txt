HOOK
India just relit a missing light in its own GPS system.

WHAT IT IS
ISRO's GSLV-F18 rocket launched NVS-03, a navigation satellite for NavIC, India's own positioning system.

ANALOGY
Picture lighthouses along a coast. A ship must see at least four lights to fix its position. One light went dark, and this launch relights it.

HOW IT WORKS
First, the rocket placed the 2,250 kilogram satellite in a transfer orbit 18 minutes after liftoff. Second, the satellite will raise itself to its final orbit, carrying an Indian-made atomic clock.

BEFORE
Before, NavIC had only three working satellites, one short of the minimum for standalone positioning.

AFTER
Now ISRO's chairman says the launch restores the minimum four, and a new L1 signal makes NavIC easier for everyday phones.

WHY IT MATTERS
India gains a navigation system it controls. Orbit-raising is still ahead. Listed suppliers like MTAR are indirect. Our judgment: watch. Not financial advice.
