WHAT IT IS
Silicon photonics reaches high-volume factories.

WHAT IT DOES
ST says its PIC100 silicon-photonics process entered high-volume production on 300 mm lines for hyperscalers (800G and 1.6T optical links), with plans to more than quadruple capacity by 2027. Think of it like this: The lab recipe is now on the supermarket shelf.

The proof point that photonics research becomes revenue: ST is at volume.

AFTER
