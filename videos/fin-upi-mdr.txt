FrontierRadar explainer: India delays a 0.4% UPI merchant fee to 'immediately after Diwali'
Issue: 2026-10-30 | Stage 2 · Pilot | Readiness 66/100 | Voice: Piper en_US-lessac-medium (local)

HOOK
India's phone payment system may start charging shops a tiny fee on big payments, which could help payment companies earn money.

WHAT IT IS
India delays a 0.4% UPI merchant fee to 'immediately after Diwali'.

WHAT IT DOES
NPCI said a 0.4% merchant discount rate on person-to-merchant UPI payments above ₹2,000 at medium and large merchants, capped at ₹300, will start 'immediately post Diwali' on a date to be notified; it was due on Oct 15. Think of it like this: Like a free highway adding a small toll only for big trucks, to pay for repairs.

BEFORE
UPI has been free for merchants.

AFTER
Who could benefit: UPI payment apps and payment aggregators and Banks in the UPI chain.

WHY IT MATTERS
We rate it Stage 2, Pilot, readiness 66 out of 100. The catch: Already postponed once, and the Supreme Court is reviewing it. Our judgment: watch. Not financial advice.
