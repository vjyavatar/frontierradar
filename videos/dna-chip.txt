HOOK
Making DNA for storage is slow.

WHAT IT IS
A porous silica chip writes 11× more DNA per spot, a step to cheaper 'DNA drives'.

WHAT IT DOES
A porous silica thin film raises on-chip DNA yield to more than 1,821 pmol per cm², over 11 times a flat surface, using chip-making-compatible electrochemistry. Think of it like this: Like turning a flat parking lot into a multi-storey garage: same footprint, many more cars.

BEFORE
Writing DNA is the costliest step in DNA storage.

AFTER
Who could benefit: Twist Bioscience TWST and Semiconductor foundries.

WHY IT MATTERS
We rate it Stage 1, Lab proof, readiness 34 out of 100. The catch: Demonstrated with 121-letter strands in a lab-built system. Our judgment: watch. Not financial advice.
