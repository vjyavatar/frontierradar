HOOK
What if you could rebuild a chip after you bought it?

WHAT IT IS
Altera has launched its Agilex 2X family of FPGA chips, led by Agilex 27, for AI infrastructure, networking and edge devices.

WHAT IT DOES
An FPGA is a chip you can re-wire after you buy it, like Lego you can rebuild for each new AI job.

BEFORE
AI networking and pre-processing change too fast for custom chips, so FPGAs fill those gaps. The previous generation used more power for the same work.

AFTER
Altera claims 1.8 times better performance per watt. Altera and network and telecom equipment makers could benefit.

WHY IT MATTERS
We rate it Stage 2, Prototype, with a readiness score of 67 out of 100. The figure is vendor-claimed, and Altera is private, so there is no direct listed stock. Our judgment: watch for customer design wins and shipment dates. Not financial advice.
