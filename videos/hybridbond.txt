FrontierRadar explainer: Gluing chips together copper-to-copper (hybrid bonding)
Issue: 2026-10-30 | Stage 3 · Factories & orders | Readiness 80/100 | Voice: Piper en_US-lessac-medium (local)

HOOK
Next AI chips will be fused copper to copper.

WHAT IT IS
Hybrid bonding joins chips directly, pad to pad.

ANALOGY
Think of a sweater knitted in one piece instead of panels stitched with bulky seams. With no seams, the fabric is thinner, lighter and stronger, and the pattern lines up perfectly.

HOW IT WORKS
First, the chip surfaces are made extremely flat and clean. Second, copper pad meets copper pad and fuses, with no solder bump in between.

BEFORE
Before, stacked chips were joined with tiny solder bumps, which take space between layers.

AFTER
Now toolmakers are teaming up, but SK hynix says it is not ready for HBM4E.

WHY IT MATTERS
The timing slip favours legacy bonder makers short term. Besi and Applied Materials longer term, and Adeia as an IP licensor, are the exposure. Our judgment: watch. Not financial advice.

ANALOGY MAP (in the analogy -> in reality)
- Stitched seams -> Solder bumps
- Knitted in one piece -> Copper fused to copper
- Thinner fabric -> Tighter chip stacks
