FrontierRadar explainer: One transistor that can be logic, memory or neuron
Issue: 2026-10-30 | Stage 1 · Paper | Readiness 16/100 | Voice: Piper en_US-lessac-medium (local)

HOOK
One transistor can now switch jobs.

WHAT IT IS
It is an atom-thin transistor that acts as logic, synapse or neuron.

ANALOGY
Think of a football utility player. The coach can move him to defence, midfield or attack during the same match, so the squad needs fewer players.

HOW IT WORKS
First, the transistor is reassigned to a new role. Second, working as a filter, it drops useless data before the brain-like part wastes energy on it.

BEFORE
Before, logic, memory and neuron jobs used separate kinds of devices.

AFTER
Now one atom-thin device, from researchers in Singapore, can be reassigned between all three.

WHY IT MATTERS
It is pure research, roughly a decade from products, with no investable pure play yet. Our judgment: wait. Not financial advice.
