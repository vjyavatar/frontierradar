FrontierRadar explainer: Computing with light instead of electricity (optical AI chip)
Issue: 2026-10-30 | Stage 2 · Prototype | Readiness 31/100 | Voice: Piper en_US-lessac-medium (local)

HOOK
Some computers might one day think with flashlight beams instead of electricity, so they stay cool and fast.

WHAT IT IS
Computing with light instead of electricity.

WHAT IT DOES
A startup is building chips that do AI math with light passing through tiny 'metamaterial' patterns, and is working with MIT on the software to run them. Think of it like this: Like sorting mail by shining a torch through a stencil instead of reading every envelope.

BEFORE
Optical AI compute is still pre-revenue but backed by granted IP and an MIT software partnership.

AFTER
Who could benefit: Neurophos and GlobalFoundries GFS.

WHY IT MATTERS
We rate it Stage 2, Prototype, readiness 31 out of 100. The catch: Nature Photonics review: in the cloud, losses and light-to-electric conversions 'dominate power' and have so far prevented commercial photonic tensor cores. Our judgment: watch. Not financial advice.
