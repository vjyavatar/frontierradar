FrontierRadar explainer: Gluing chips together copper-to-copper (hybrid bonding)
Issue: 2026-10-30 | Stage 3 · Factories & orders | Readiness 80/100 | Voice: Piper en_US-lessac-medium (local)

HOOK
Instead of using tiny solder balls, chips are pressed together so their copper parts fuse, like welding.

WHAT IT IS
Gluing chips together copper-to-copper.

WHAT IT DOES
Tool makers are teaming up to sell machines that fuse chips directly, copper pad to copper pad. Think of it like this: Welding Lego bricks instead of snapping them: tighter, but harder to do right.

BEFORE
Hybrid bonding is at the orders stage.

AFTER
Who could benefit: Besi BESI.AS and Applied Materials AMAT.

WHY IT MATTERS
We rate it Stage 3, Factories & orders, readiness 80 out of 100. The catch: SK hynix says hybrid bonding won't be ready for HBM4E memory stacks. Our judgment: watch. Not financial advice.
