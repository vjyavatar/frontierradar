FrontierRadar explainer: EU's HELENA project makes halide solid-state battery pouch cells
Issue: 2026-10-30 | Stage 2 · Pilot cell | Readiness 41/100 | Voice: Piper en_US-lessac-medium (local)

HOOK
Europe's HELENA project is making solid-state battery cells with factory methods.

WHAT IT IS
HELENA built halide solid-state pouch cells using scalable steps.

ANALOGY
Think of a luthier who hand-built one guitar, now setting up a workshop line with jigs so every guitar comes out the same.

HOW IT WORKS
First, extrusion makes high-loading cathodes, about 4.5 to 5 milliamp hours per square centimetre. Second, thin halide separators reach about 4 millisiemens per centimetre.

BEFORE
Before, most solid-state papers stopped at lab cells.

AFTER
Now there are 40 milliamp hour pouch cells made with manufacturing steps.

WHY IT MATTERS
Still far smaller than EV cells. European battery makers are the exposure. Our judgment: watch. Not financial advice.
