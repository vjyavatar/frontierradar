HOOK
Scientists store files in DNA.

WHAT IT IS
HKU's 'Gungnir' code recovers data from DNA damaged like 400-year-old archives.

WHAT IT DOES
A blockchain-inspired error-correction method uses computing power to guess and verify the original data. Think of it like this: Like rebuilding a torn, faded letter by trying millions of guesses and checking each against a fingerprint until one fits.

BEFORE
DNA can hold huge amounts of data for centuries, but the molecules decay and reading them adds errors.

AFTER
Who could benefit: DNA synthesis and sequencing toolmakers and Archive and cloud storage providers.
