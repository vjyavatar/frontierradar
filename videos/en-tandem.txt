HOOK
Special solar cells catch light on both sides.

WHAT IT IS
Two-sided perovskite-silicon solar cells keep 90% efficiency after 1,000 hot hours.

WHAT IT DOES
The team identified 'lattice compliance', how well the crystal flexes under stress, as a key to heat-and-light stability. Think of it like this: Like choosing a bridge that flexes in the wind instead of cracking.

BEFORE
Perovskite-silicon tandems promise more power per panel, but perovskites degrade.

AFTER
Who could benefit: Solar manufacturers developing tandems.

WHY IT MATTERS
We rate it Stage 1, Lab cell, readiness 32 out of 100. The catch: 1,000 hours is far below panel lifetimes. Our judgment: watch. Not financial advice.
