HOOK
India's central bank is telling banks to lock their digital side doors.

WHAT IT IS
Reuters reports the R B I asked banks to check weak spots in A P Is, the links that connect them to fintechs and lenders.

ANALOGY
Picture a housing society that finds thieves entering through the service lift used by delivery partners. Now the guard checks every partner's key card.

HOW IT WORKS
First, banks must probe their A P I links for weaknesses. Second, they must show proof of compliance and readiness for A I risks.

BEFORE
Before, rules existed, but sources say banks were slow to apply them. Two lenders lost about 2 million dollars through a hacked A P I link.

AFTER
Now the regulator is asking for proof, not promises.

WHY IT MATTERS
Security spending may rise; small lenders face higher costs. This is based on unnamed sources. Our judgment: watch. Not financial advice.
