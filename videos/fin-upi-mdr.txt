FrontierRadar explainer: India delays a 0.4% UPI merchant fee to 'immediately after Diwali'
Issue: 2026-10-30 | Stage 2 · Pilot | Readiness 66/100 | Voice: Piper en_US-lessac-medium (local)

HOOK
India delayed a 0.4 percent UPI fee until after Diwali.

WHAT IT IS
It is a merchant fee on larger UPI payments.

ANALOGY
Think of a free museum that adds a small fee only for big tour groups, with a maximum charge, while single visitors still walk in free.

HOW IT WORKS
First, a 0.4 percent fee applies to payments above 2,000 rupees at medium and large merchants, capped at 300 rupees. Second, it starts right after Diwali, on a date to be notified.

BEFORE
Before, UPI was free for merchants.

AFTER
Now the fee is delayed from October 15, and the Supreme Court asked for affidavits. UPI handled 24.07 billion transactions in September.

WHY IT MATTERS
Payment apps and banks could gain revenue. Our judgment: watch. Not financial advice.
