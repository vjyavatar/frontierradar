HOOK
What if an AI chip ran on light?

WHAT IT IS
Neurophos is a startup doing AI math by shining light through tiny metamaterial patterns.

ANALOGY
Think of a cathedral stained-glass window. Sunlight passes through and the colours sort themselves instantly, with no effort.

HOW IT WORKS
First, data is encoded into light. Second, the patterns transform it as it passes, doing the calculation.

BEFORE
Before, AI math meant pushing electricity through transistors, creating heat.

AFTER
Now there is granted IP and an MIT software partnership, but no product yet.

WHY IT MATTERS
Photonics foundries like GlobalFoundries and Tower are the picks-and-shovels play. Our judgment: watch. Not financial advice.
