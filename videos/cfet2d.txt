FrontierRadar explainer: Stacking atom-thin transistors on top of each other (2D CFETs)
Issue: 2026-10-30 | Stage 1 · Paper | Readiness 16/100 | Voice: Piper en_US-lessac-medium (local)

HOOK
Europe's top chip lab tested stacked atom-thin transistors and said: not yet.

WHAT IT IS
imec studied 2D CFETs for the A2 node.

ANALOGY
Think of a double-decker bus. It carries more people per road, but if the stairs and doors are narrow, boarding is so slow you lose the gain.

HOW IT WORKS
First, imec compared stacked atom-thin transistors to silicon. Second, it measured the contact and wiring losses.

BEFORE
Before, hopes were high for 2D materials.

AFTER
Now the honest answer is they do not yet beat silicon.

WHY IT MATTERS
A 2030s story. Toolmakers ASML, Applied and Lam win either way. Our judgment: wait. Not financial advice.
