HOOK
PsiQuantum built a light switch that rewires AI data-centre networks in 3 milliseconds.

WHAT IT IS
It is an 8 by 8 optical circuit switch on a silicon-photonics chip.

ANALOGY
Think of a city junction. With traffic lights every car stops; a flyover lets traffic pass without stopping.

HOW IT WORKS
First, light enters the chip's ultra-low-loss waveguides. Second, the switch steers it straight to a new port.

BEFORE
Before, switches converted light to electricity and back, costing power and delay.

AFTER
Now PsiQuantum reports 1.2 decibels loss and 40 decibels crosstalk, and samples are with select customers.

WHY IT MATTERS
Company-reported. GlobalFoundries and hyperscalers could gain. Our judgment: watch. Not financial advice.
