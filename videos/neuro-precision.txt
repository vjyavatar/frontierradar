WHAT IT IS
It is a neural atlas of more than 180 hours recorded with temporary surface arrays.

ANALOGY
Think of writing a dictionary. One speaker gives you a few words; 70 speakers give you the shared language everyone uses.

HOW IT WORKS
First, thin Layer 7 arrays rest on the brain surface without penetrating it. Second, pooled recordings train decoders for movement, speech and facial expression.

BEFORE
Before, decoders were trained patient by patient.

AFTER
Now 70 patients feed one atlas, and Precision opened its first international site, in Abu Dhabi.

WHY IT MATTERS
Shared data could speed decoder training. Recordings are temporary, not permanent implants, and the company is private. Our judgment: watch. Not financial advice.
