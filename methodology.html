📐 Methodology

How we score: open recipe, no black box

Every number on FrontierRadar comes from one published formula. Here it is, with a worked example and its limits.

Why a formula?

So you can check our work. Every score on this site comes from the same recipe below. Anyone with the sources can reproduce it, and argue with our inputs.

1. Readiness (0-100): how close to making money

CriterionWeightHow we score it (0-5)
Evidence level55%How far along the proof is (TRL-style ladder).
Manufacturability20%0 = exotic materials no fab can make; 5 = runs on today's standard lines.
Supply chain10%0 = tools/materials don't exist; 5 = mature suppliers ready.
Corporate backing10%0 = academic only; 2 = funded startup; 3 = big-company R&D; 5 = several big firms committing capex.
Independent groups5%1 = one lab; 5 = industry-wide effort.

Evidence ladder (the biggest weight)

  1. Paper / simulation only → 0% of the evidence weight · Stage 1 · years-to-market 6-10
  2. Lab demo → 10% of the evidence weight · Stage 1 · years-to-market 4-8
  3. Working prototype chip → 25% of the evidence weight · Stage 2 · years-to-market 3-6
  4. Customer samples / qualification → 45% of the evidence weight · Stage 2 · years-to-market 2-4
  5. Volume production or orders → 75% of the evidence weight · Stage 3 · years-to-market 0-2
  6. Revenue at scale → 100% of the evidence weight · Stage 4 · years-to-market now

Readiness = sum of weight × (sub-score ÷ 5). Evidence uses the ladder % instead, because going from samples to volume is a far bigger jump than from paper to demo.

2. Other scores

🧪 Stage

Set by evidence only: 0-1 → Stage 1 Paper, 2-3 → Stage 2 Prototype, 4 → Stage 3 Factories & orders, 5 → Stage 4 Revenue.

⏳ Years to market

From the evidence ladder. Early items (evidence 0-2) get +2 years if manufacturability is 0-1, or −2 years if it already runs on standard lines (4-5).

🎢 Hype vs Reality

Hype = 10 × claim boldness (0-5; "1000× faster" = 5) + 10 × attention (0-5: press, funding, famous backers). Gap = Hype − Readiness: up to 10 = reality catching up, up to 35 = some hype, more = mostly hype.

⚖️ Patent / IP risk

Average of crowding (how many patent holders, 0-5) and litigation history (0-5): 3.5+ High, 2+ Medium, else Low. Not legal advice.

🎯 Conviction (opportunities)

Score = 0.4 × average readiness of linked research + 0.4 × 20 × exposure (share of the business riding on it, 0-5) + 0.2 × 20 × execution (track record, balance sheet, 0-5). 65+ High, 45-64 Medium, under 45 Speculative. Not directly investable, or exposure 0-1 → Watch. Execution 0-1 (e.g. pre-profit, funding risk) caps it at Speculative.

5. Data sources & update cadence

  • Papers: Nature-family journals, arXiv, AIP and conference proceedings (IEDM, ISSCC).
  • Patents: Google Patents and patent-analytics sites.
  • Company facts: SEC filings, investor-relations releases, earnings reports.
  • Counter-evidence: trade press, reviews and company filings, always linked in each "What could go wrong" box.
  • Cadence: one issue per day when there is news. Each item keeps first-seen, last-updated and history, and every call is logged on the Track record.

6. Limitations (please read)

  • Sub-scores are human judgment. Two careful analysts could differ by ±1 on any criterion (about ±10 readiness points).
  • Weights are our choice, not a statistically validated model. Any change to them will be published here.
  • Press releases overstate; papers under-report failures. A missing bear source does not mean low risk.
  • We have no access to private company data, customer contracts or patent claim charts.
  • Conviction is about the research-to-revenue story, not share price or valuation.

⚡ Urgency signal

Each item gets one of three badges. It says how time-sensitive the item looks to us, not whether it will make money.

  • Score U = ½ × readiness + 15 if a dated catalyst within 90 days names the developer or a linked company + 3 × positive readiness momentum − 8 × each kill-risk flag (bold claim ≥4, IP litigation ≥3, or still Stage 1).
  • 🔥 Act now U ≥ 55, readiness ≥ 70, a listed ticker is linked and a catalyst falls within 90 days.
  • ⏳ Build position U ≥ 40, readiness ≥ 55 and a listed ticker is linked.
  • 👀 Watch everything else, including all items with no listed company.
