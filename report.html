Deep-Tech Frontier Intelligence: AI Hardware

Sample weekly issue, Oct 28, 2026. Five AI-hardware signals across photonics, in-memory compute and advanced packaging, each with a readiness score, patent evidence, global stock exposure and green/red impact.

Executive briefing

Bottom line. The money and research this month went into the memory and interconnect wall, not raw compute. Photonic startups now hold real patent estates. Packaging is the layer closest to revenue, and India's listed OSATs are the local way in.
  1. Closest to revenue: Applied Materials + Besi hybrid bonding (granted US patents in 2026), plus glass substrates aiming for a production line by end-2027.
  2. Strongest IP surprise: Volantis has 26 published US applications and 3 grants for a photonic wafer-scale interposer.
  3. IBM's Oct 2026 arXiv paper has a matching patent application, US 2026/0291719 A1, published Sep 24, 2026, by the same authors.
  4. India: CG Power (CG Semi) and Kaynes (Kaynes Semicon) are the listed names with OSAT plants in commercial production. Tata Electronics is not listed.
  5. Readiness scores are analyst judgment. IP notes are informational, not legal advice. Nothing here is investment advice.

1. Neurophos optical processing unit

What
MIT CSAIL's Julia Lab and Neurophos announced a collaboration (Oct 21) to run numerical algorithms on Neurophos' Optical Processing Unit, an optical systolic array for matrix math.
Key claims
Metasurface modulators about 10,000x smaller than Mach-Zehnder devices, with millions of elements in 5x5 mm. The company states a goal of about 50x lower compute resource use. These are company claims.
Why it matters
Optical inference could undercut the energy each GPU operation costs, which is the binding cost in data centers.
Readiness
4/10. The architecture works and is patented, but commercial systems are planned only for 2028.
Patents (verified)
US 11,120,332 B2, granted Sep 14, 2021. US 11,843,182 B2, granted Dec 12, 2023. WO 2021/026152 A1 and EP 4007888 (priority Aug 4, 2019). Assignee: Neurophos.
Who / tickers
Neurophos (private). GlobalFoundries (GFS) is linked only through a summit talk.
Source type
Primary: university announcement plus patent records.

2. Volantis photonic memory interposer

What
Volantis, an Altman-backed startup, is building an accelerator with a photonic interposer that links memory to compute using directly modulated VCSELs and on-chip waveguides.
Key claims
Upwards of 10 TB of memory at up to 240 TB/s per package, according to The Register (Oct 5). Earlier, the company claimed 1 pJ/bit links. These are company claims.
Why it matters
It attacks the memory wall directly and could challenge HBM plus electrical interposers.
Readiness
4/10. The patent estate is real, but the MVP is still 12–18 months out according to reporting.
Patents (verified)
Assignee Volantis Semiconductor Inc.: 26 published applications and 3 grants (latest grant Aug 25, 2026). Examples: US 2026/0119385 A1 (disaggregated memory on a photonic wafer-scale interposer), US 2026/0121756 A1 (quantum-dot VCSEL interposer), US 2026/0219465 A1 (optical wafer-scale switch).
Who / tickers
Volantis (private). No listed partner was named.
Source type
Press interview, cross-checked against patent filings. No peer-reviewed paper was found.

3. IBM superposed analog memory

What
arXiv 2610.26118: many vectors are stored in superposition in one analog memory array and recovered with an in-memory matrix-vector multiply.
Key result
Run on a prototype phase-change memory chip, on recommendation-model and LLM embedding tables. Accuracy was comparable to full-precision software.
Why it matters
It adds a new scaling knob: more effective storage density without more memory cells.
Readiness
3/10. A real-silicon prototype exists, but there is no product roadmap.
Patents (verified)
US 2026/0291719 A1, 'Compressed Memory Using Vector Superposition', filed Mar 24, 2025, published Sep 24, 2026. Applicant IBM. Same inventors as the paper. Background: US 10,678,885 B2 (IBM, in-memory matrix-vector multiply).
Who / tickers
IBM (IBM). Related noise-handling work by Micron (MU) authors: arXiv 2610.20359.
Source type
Primary: preprint plus patent application.

4. 200G slow-light silicon modulator

What
Nature Communications (Oct 22): a 320 µm silicon photonic-crystal slow-light modulator with a built-in electro-optic equalizer.
Key result
140 Gb/s OOK at 26 fJ/bit with CMOS-level drive. 180 Gb/s PAM4 without DSP, and 200 Gb/s with an 11-tap equalizer.
Why it matters
It cuts DSP and driver power, a route to dense co-packaged optics for AI clusters.
Readiness
4/10. Lab device only; it is not yet in a co-packaged optics product.
Patents
No patent from this author group was verified in this run. Prior work: Baba group at Yokohama National University, with NTT-affiliated co-authors. Run a family search before diligence.
Who / tickers
The paper names no company commercializing it. Co-packaged optics is in scope for the AMAT–Besi program below.
Source type
Primary: peer-reviewed journal.

5. Hybrid bonding and glass substrates

What
Applied Materials and Besi expanded their partnership on Oct 1. Besi joins Applied's EPIC Center to work on die-to-wafer hybrid bonding, TCB scaling, die-on-panel and co-packaged optics. Their jointly developed Kinex system is already on sale.
Glass cores
Samsung Electro-Mechanics' 100x100 mm glass substrates are in package-level verification, targeting a production line by end-2027. BOE's 24-layer glass substrates are in AI-chip packaging trials.
Why it matters
Large multi-die GPU+HBM packages warp on organic substrates. Glass substrates and hybrid bonding are the way to keep scaling.
Readiness
7/10. A product is shipping (Kinex) and substrates are in customer verification.
Patents (verified)
US 12,550,662 B2, hybrid bonding process flow, filed Nov 18, 2022, granted Feb 10, 2026. US 12,538,855, hybrid bonding process sequencing, filed Dec 9, 2022, gazetted Jan 27, 2026. Both assigned to Applied Materials.
Who / tickers
AMAT (Nasdaq), BESI (Euronext Amsterdam), Samsung Electro-Mechanics (Korea-listed), BOE (China-listed).
Source type
Company press release, product page and patent grants. No academic paper is needed: this is an industry track.
