FrontierRadar explainer: Blue Origin plans new Florida launch pads to raise New Glenn's flight rate
Issue: 2026-10-30 | Stage 3 · Flight | Readiness 70/100 | Voice: Piper en_US-lessac-medium (local)

HOOK
Blue Origin is fixing its launch pad after an accident and building more pads to launch more often.

WHAT IT IS
Blue Origin plans new Florida launch pads to raise New Glenn's flight rate.

WHAT IT DOES
Blue Origin is rebuilding pad LC-36A after a May static-fire explosion, expects it ready by early December, targets a New Glenn return to flight this year, and plans new Florida pads for a higher flight rate. Think of it like this: Like a bakery rebuilding its oven after a fire while planning two more ovens.

BEFORE
More heavy-lift capacity means more competition and capacity for satellite constellations.

AFTER
Who could benefit: Blue Origin and Satellite operators needing launches.

WHY IT MATTERS
We rate it Stage 3, Flight, readiness 70 out of 100. The catch: Return-to-flight timing depends on pad rebuild. Our judgment: wait. Not financial advice.
