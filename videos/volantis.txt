FrontierRadar explainer: A light-based 'highway' between AI chip and memory
Issue: 2026-10-30 | Stage 1 · Paper | Readiness 32/100 | Voice: Piper en_US-lessac-medium (local)

HOOK
AI chips are waiting on memory. Volantis wants to move that data by light.

WHAT IT IS
It is a plan for optical links built into the interposer under an AI chip and its memory.

ANALOGY
Think of an old post office that switched from bicycle couriers to pneumatic tubes running under the building.

HOW IT WORKS
First, lasers turn data into light. Second, the light travels through the base layer to memory.

BEFORE
Before, the memory wall slowed AI chips.

AFTER
Now there is a plan, not a product.

WHY IT MATTERS
High risk, but laser makers Coherent and Lumentum gain whichever startup wins. Our judgment: wait. Not financial advice.
