HOOK
Blue Origin plans more Florida launch pads to fly New Glenn more often.

WHAT IT IS
It is a plan to rebuild one pad and add new ones.

ANALOGY
Think of a busy railway station adding platforms. More platforms means more trains per day.

HOW IT WORKS
First, Blue Origin rebuilds pad LC-36A after a May static-fire explosion, expected ready by early December. Second, it adds new pads.

BEFORE
Before, one damaged pad limited New Glenn.

AFTER
Now Blue Origin targets a return to flight this year.

WHY IT MATTERS
More heavy-lift capacity helps satellite constellations. Blue Origin is private. Our judgment: wait. Not financial advice.
