HOOK
A light brain chip is reported over a thousand times faster on vision.

WHAT IT IS
It uses optical devices that act as synapse and neuron.

ANALOGY
Think of a sprint start. Normally the ear hears the gun and then the legs react. Imagine a starting block that both senses the gun and launches the runner in one action.

HOW IT WORKS
First, light carries the signal into chip-compatible optical devices. Second, the same device weighs the signal like a synapse and fires like a neuron.

BEFORE
Before, standard designs had more delay and energy.

AFTER
Now delay fell 1,176 times and energy 239 times in their tests.

WHY IT MATTERS
