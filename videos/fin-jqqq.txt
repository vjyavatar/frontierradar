HOOK
A Nasdaq-100 fund now lives on a blockchain.

WHAT IT IS
Janus Henderson's J Q Q Q is an issuer-sponsored on-chain fund.

ANALOGY
Think of concert tickets moving from paper at the box office to a phone wallet. The key: the venue itself issues them, not a reseller.

HOW IT WORKS
First, Janus Henderson and Centrifuge issue the fund on the Stellar blockchain. Second, Nasdaq licenses the index, unlike third-party stock tokens.

BEFORE
Before, tokenization mostly covered cash-like money-market funds.

AFTER
Now a stock index is on-chain, taking Janus Henderson's platform above 1 billion dollars.

WHY IT MATTERS
Still small. Asset managers and index providers are exposure. Our judgment: watch. Not financial advice.
