FrontierRadar explainer: Janus Henderson puts a Nasdaq-100 fund on a blockchain (JQQQ)
Issue: 2026-10-30 | Stage 3 · Live launch | Readiness 74/100 | Voice: Piper en_US-lessac-medium (local)

HOOK
A big fund company made a version of its fund that lives on the internet's shared ledger, so it can move faster.

WHAT IT IS
Janus Henderson puts a Nasdaq-100 fund on a blockchain.

WHAT IT DOES
Janus Henderson and Centrifuge launched JQQQ, an issuer-sponsored on-chain fund on the Stellar blockchain that tracks the Nasdaq-100. Think of it like this: Like moving a mutual fund from paper certificates to a digital ticket that can change hands any hour of the day.

BEFORE
Tokenization had mostly covered cash-like money-market funds.

AFTER
Who could benefit: Asset managers with on-chain platforms and Index providers licensing to tokenized products.

WHY IT MATTERS
We rate it Stage 3, Live launch, readiness 74 out of 100. The catch: Assets are tiny versus the ETF market; demand not yet proven. Our judgment: watch. Not financial advice.
