Issue: 2026-10-30 | Stage 2 · Prototype | Readiness 52/100 | Voice: Piper en_US-lessac-medium (local)

HOOK
The board under a chip may switch from plastic to glass, because glass stays flat and doesn't warp.

WHAT IT IS
Glass replacing plastic under AI chips.

WHAT IT DOES
Several firms are testing glass as the base board under giant AI chips because it stays flat and lets wires sit closer together. Think of it like this: Building a skyscraper on granite instead of plywood.

BEFORE
Glass substrates are moving from lab to pilot lines.

AFTER
Who could benefit: Ibiden 4062.T and Samsung Electro-Mechanics 009150.KS.

WHY IT MATTERS
We rate it Stage 2, Prototype, readiness 52 out of 100. The catch: Tom's Hardware: glass-core substrates are 'still not in a single commercial product' and every timeline has slipped. Our judgment: watch. Not financial advice.
