Issue: 2026-10-31 | Stage 3 · Generally available | Readiness 81/100 | Voice: Piper en_US-lessac-medium (local)

HOOK
A trillion-parameter AI model is now free to download.

WHAT IT IS
Mistral released the weights of Mistral Large 4, its biggest open model, built for coding and cyber defence.

ANALOGY
Think of a carmaker publishing its full engine design. Any garage can build and tune the engine, but taxi firms selling rides with it need a separate licence.

HOW IT WORKS
First, organisations download the model and run it on their own hardware. Second, they fine-tune it on private data, for example security logs.

BEFORE
Before, most top models could only be rented through an online service.

AFTER
Now Mistral says it leads open models on two coding tests, at 70 cents per million input tokens through its API.

WHY IT MATTERS
Banks and governments wanting control get a strong option. Benchmarks are Mistral's own. Our judgment: watch. Not financial advice.
