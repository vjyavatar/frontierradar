HOOK
Micron found a simple fix for sloppy analog AI chips.

WHAT IT IS
It is weight splitting: spreading each AI weight across several memory cells.

ANALOGY
Think of Olympic diving judges. One judge may be off, but averaging several gives a fair score.

HOW IT WORKS
First, each weight is stored across several cells. Second, their errors average out.

BEFORE
Before, analog chips were efficient but unreliable.

AFTER
Now accuracy improves with a simple method.

WHY IT MATTERS
