HOOK
Meta just gave away what it calls the most capable US open-weight model.

WHAT IT IS
Muse Spark Open is a model whose weights anyone can download.

ANALOGY
Think of an architect publishing full blueprints. Anyone can build the house on their own land, and change the rooms.

HOW IT WORKS
First, companies download the weights. Second, they run and adapt the model on their own servers.

BEFORE
Before, many firms had to send data to someone else's cloud model.

AFTER
Now they can run Meta's model in-house. The most-capable claim is Meta's own.

WHY IT MATTERS
Open weights help Meta's ecosystem and firms running AI on their own hardware. Our judgment: watch. Not financial advice.
