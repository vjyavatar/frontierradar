HOOK
Making transistors from sheets just one atom thick and stacking them like pancakes.

WHAT IT IS
Stacking atom-thin transistors on top of each other.

WHAT IT DOES
imec tested whether transistors made of atom-thin materials and stacked in pairs beat silicon at the ~2026+ 'A2' node. Think of it like this: A thinner wall doesn't help if the doors and hallways are the bottleneck.

BEFORE
A sober reality-check paper: 2D CFETs are a 2030s story.

AFTER
Who could benefit: ASML and Applied Materials AMAT.

WHY IT MATTERS
We rate it Stage 1, Paper, readiness 16 out of 100. The catch: No fab today grows 2D-material layers at wafer scale with chip-grade yield. Our judgment: watch. Not financial advice.
