HOOK

WHAT IT IS
Making noisy analog AI chips accurate.

WHAT IT DOES
Micron showed that splitting each AI 'weight' across several memory cells averages out the errors that make analog chips unreliable. Think of it like this: Asking three friends to estimate a distance and averaging: fewer big mistakes.

BEFORE
A memory giant publishing fixes for analog AI errors suggests in-memory compute is moving toward products.

AFTER
Who could benefit: Micron MU and Kaynes KAYNES.NS.

WHY IT MATTERS
