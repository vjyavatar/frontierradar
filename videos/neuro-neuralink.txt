HOOK
Neuralink trained its brain decoder on over 50,000 hours of recordings.

WHAT IT IS
It is a pretrained decoder for brain implants, the way AI models are pretrained.

ANALOGY
Think of a navigation app. It does not learn your city from your drives alone; it learns from millions of drivers, so it works on day one.

HOW IT WORKS
First, the decoder learns general patterns from pooled recordings. Second, each user only fine-tunes it.

BEFORE
Before, users needed frequent recalibration, some daily.

AFTER
Now some recalibrate weekly, with cursor control reaching 11.32 bits per second.

WHY IT MATTERS
Easier daily life makes implants more practical. Company-reported, and Neuralink is private. Our judgment: watch. Not financial advice.
