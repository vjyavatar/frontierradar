HOOK
A DNA storage system just passed tests in living cells with 100 percent recovery.

WHAT IT IS
DNA-SaM is a full system for writing big files into DNA and finding them again.

ANALOGY
Picture a giant library. A floor directory sends you to the right floor, a shelf label to the right shelf, and the librarian refuses any book with a binding that falls apart.

HOW IT WORKS
First, it encodes data fast, in time that grows only in step with file size. Second, it files the pieces under a two-level index so large archives stay searchable.

BEFORE
Before, most DNA codecs were slow, or made sequences that failed in the lab.

AFTER
Now it worked in the test tube and in living cells with 100 percent success, with a theoretical capacity above 270 petabytes.

WHY IT MATTERS
Scale is the missing piece for DNA storage, and this targets it. Capacity is still theoretical. Our judgment: wait. Not financial advice.
