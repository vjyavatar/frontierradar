WHAT IT IS
Neuralink pretrains its brain decoder on 50,000 hours of recordings.

WHAT IT DOES
Neuralink reports more than 50,000 hours of human BCI recordings used to pretrain decoders, cutting some users' recalibration from daily to weekly and reaching 11.32 bits per second cursor control. Think of it like this: Like a phone keyboard that learned from millions of typists, so it works for you with far less practice.

BEFORE
BCI users have needed frequent recalibration.
