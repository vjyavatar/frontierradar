FrontierRadar explainer: DuoPD: small compute units inside HBM4 memory could make AI chatbots answer up to 2.2× faster
Issue: 2026-10-30 | Stage 1 · Paper | Readiness 27/100 | Voice: Piper en_US-lessac-medium (local)

HOOK
Why do AI chatbots type their answers one word at a time?

WHAT IT IS
DuoPD is a research design from Seoul National University. It adds small compute units inside HBM4, the stacked memory next to AI chips.

WHAT IT DOES
The GPU reads your prompt, and the memory itself writes the answer word by word. Think of a librarian who writes the answer at the shelf, instead of you running to the library for every sentence.

BEFORE
Until now, the GPU does all the work and keeps fetching data from memory. HBM4 moves the memory's base layer to a logic process, which opens room for compute.

AFTER
In simulation, answers come up to 2.23 times more throughput, with up to 37.9 percent energy saving. Memory makers SK hynix, Samsung and Micron could benefit.

WHY IT MATTERS
We rate it Stage 1, Paper, with a readiness score of 27 out of 100. It is simulation only, and needs memory makers to buy in. Our judgment: wait, and watch for real silicon. Not financial advice.
