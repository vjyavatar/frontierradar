FrontierRadar explainer: Two-sided perovskite-silicon solar cells keep 90% efficiency after 1,000 hot hours
Issue: 2026-10-30 | Stage 1 · Lab cell | Readiness 32/100 | Voice: Piper en_US-lessac-medium (local)

HOOK
Two-sided solar cells kept 90 percent efficiency after 1,000 hot hours.

WHAT IT IS
They are bifacial perovskite-silicon tandem cells with a new stability design rule.

ANALOGY
Think of a tower in an earthquake zone. Rigid towers crack; towers with flexible joints sway and survive.

HOW IT WORKS
First, the team found that how well the crystal flexes predicts stability. Second, they built two-sided tandems using that rule.

BEFORE
Before, perovskites degraded quickly, holding back tandems.

AFTER
Now the cells made 36.2 milliwatts per square centimetre and kept 90 percent after 1,000 hours at 65 degrees.

WHY IT MATTERS
1,000 hours is short against 25-year panels. Tandem solar makers are the exposure. Our judgment: watch. Not financial advice.
