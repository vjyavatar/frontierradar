HOOK
One tiny switch that can change jobs: sometimes a calculator, sometimes a brain cell.

WHAT IT IS
One transistor that can be logic, memory or neuron.

WHAT IT DOES
A shape-shifting atom-thin transistor that can be reassigned to act as logic, a synapse or a neuron, filtering out useless data before the 'brain' part wastes energy on it. Think of it like this: A Swiss-army-knife worker who becomes a gatekeeper, clerk or manager as needed.

BEFORE
Pure research; tracks a promising direction (reconfigurable, back-end-compatible devices).

AFTER
Who could benefit: Ferroelectric Memory Co and GlobalFoundries GFS.

WHY IT MATTERS
We rate it Stage 1, Paper, readiness 16 out of 100. The catch: BrainChip, a listed neuromorphic pioneer, reported only US$1.22M revenue and a US$12.0M loss for H1 2026 — commercial demand for brain-like chips is still tiny. Our judgment: watch. Not financial advice.
