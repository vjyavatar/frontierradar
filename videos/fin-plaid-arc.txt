HOOK
A company made a smart computer that looks at how you spend and save, to guess if you can pay back a loan.

WHAT IT IS
Plaid launches a transformer AI credit score it says predicts repayment 42% better.

WHAT IT DOES
In its Fall Product Release Plaid launched LendScore 2, which it says is 42% stronger at predicting ability to repay than traditional credit data alone, and LendScore Arc, its first transformer-based credit score that learns from the order and timing of transactions. Think of it like this: Like judging a driver by watching how they actually drive every day, not just by their old ticket record.

BEFORE
Credit scores mostly look at past loans.

AFTER
Who could benefit: Cash-flow lenders and fintech lenders and Banks lending to thin-file borrowers.

WHY IT MATTERS
We rate it Stage 3, Live launch, readiness 75 out of 100. The catch: Improvement numbers are Plaid's own internal tests. Our judgment: watch. Not financial advice.
