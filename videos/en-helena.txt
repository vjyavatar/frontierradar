FrontierRadar explainer: EU's HELENA project makes halide solid-state battery pouch cells
Issue: 2026-10-30 | Stage 2 · Pilot cell | Readiness 41/100 | Voice: Piper en_US-lessac-medium (local)

HOOK
European scientists learned how to build small flat batteries with a new safe material.

WHAT IT IS
EU's HELENA project makes halide solid-state battery pouch cells.

WHAT IT DOES
HELENA built 40 mAh single-layer pouch cells using scalable extrusion for high-loading cathodes (about 4.5–5 mAh/cm²) and halide electrolyte separators about 40 μm thin, with ionic conductivity near 4 mS/cm after densification. Think of it like this: Like moving a recipe from a test kitchen to a small bakery line.

BEFORE
Most solid-state battery papers stop at lab cells.

AFTER
Who could benefit: European battery makers.

WHY IT MATTERS
We rate it Stage 2, Pilot cell, readiness 41 out of 100. The catch: 40 mAh cells are tiny versus EV cells. Our judgment: watch. Not financial advice.
