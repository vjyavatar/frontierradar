FrontierRadar explainer: Glass replacing plastic under AI chips (glass substrates)
Issue: 2026-10-30 | Stage 2 · Prototype | Readiness 52/100 | Voice: Piper en_US-lessac-medium (local)

HOOK
The board under an AI chip may soon be glass.

WHAT IT IS
Glass substrates replace plastic under big AI chips.

ANALOGY
Think of a snooker table. Cheap tables use wood, which can bend over time. Pro tables use a slate bed that stays dead flat, so every shot runs true.

HOW IT WORKS
First, the glass board stays flat under a giant AI chip. Second, that flatness lets designers place wires closer together, fitting more connections.

BEFORE
Before, plastic boards made flatness and tight wiring hard under giant chips.

AFTER
Now firms are moving glass from lab to pilot lines, with India's Odisha project too.

WHY IT MATTERS
TSMC is testing it with Japan's Ibiden, and Samsung and Korea's SKC are racing too. Those substrate makers are the exposure. Our judgment: watch. Not financial advice.
