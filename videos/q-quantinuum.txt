Issue: 2026-10-30 | Stage 2 · Hardware demo | Readiness 54/100 | Voice: Piper en_US-lessac-medium (local)

HOOK
Quantinuum says its quantum computer now makes just five errors per million.

WHAT IT IS
It is a new error-correction record on Helios, a commercial trapped-ion machine.

ANALOGY
Picture a choir where every note is sung by a small group. If one singer goes off-key, the group's sound still carries the right note, and the conductor can hear who slipped.

HOW IT WORKS
First, information is spread across many ions using a colour code. Second, checks catch and correct errors before they spread.

BEFORE
Before, its own September record had 10 times more errors.

AFTER
Now it reports 99.9995 percent logical fidelity, in the target range for Apollo, a fault-tolerant machine planned by 2029.

WHY IT MATTERS
Error correction decides when quantum computers become useful, and Helios is sold today. The record is company-reported. Our judgment: small bet. Not financial advice.
