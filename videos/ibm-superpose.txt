FrontierRadar explainer: Storing many values in one memory spot (superposed analog memory)
Issue: 2026-10-30 | Stage 1 · Paper | Readiness 19/100 | Voice: Piper en_US-lessac-medium (local)

HOOK
IBM packed several values into one memory spot.

WHAT IT IS
It is superposed analog memory, for AI search.

ANALOGY
Think of a radio tower carrying many stations on one antenna. Your radio tunes to the one you want.

HOW IT WORKS
First, several pieces of information are written into the same analog cell. Second, they are read back separately.

BEFORE
Before, each value needed its own memory spot.

AFTER
Now less memory is needed for AI search, and IBM has filed patents.

WHY IT MATTERS
Years away. IBM, Micron and Weebit Nano are exposure. Our judgment: wait. Not financial advice.
