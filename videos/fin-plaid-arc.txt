HOOK
Plaid says its AI credit score predicts repayment 42 percent better.

WHAT IT IS
LendScore uses bank transactions to judge borrowers.

ANALOGY
Think of a weather forecaster. Monthly averages tell you little about tomorrow. A live radar sequence, showing the order and timing of clouds, tells you much more.

HOW IT WORKS
First, LendScore Arc, a transformer model, reads the order and timing of bank transactions. Second, it turns that pattern into a repayment score.

BEFORE
Before, scores mostly looked at past loans.

AFTER
Now Plaid says LendScore 2 is 42 percent stronger than traditional credit data alone, which helps people with thin credit files.

WHY IT MATTERS
These are Plaid's internal results, not independent tests. Cash-flow and fintech lenders are the exposure. Our judgment: watch. Not financial advice.
