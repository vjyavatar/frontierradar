HOOK
Chips wait for memory like kids waiting in a lunch line.

WHAT IT IS
A light-based 'highway' between AI chip and memory.

WHAT IT DOES
Volantis revealed a plan to build optical connections into the base layer (interposer) that sits under an AI chip and its memory, so data moves by light. Think of it like this: Replacing a crowded two-lane road between factory and warehouse with a bullet train.

BEFORE
Early-stage photonic interposer aimed at the 'memory wall'.

AFTER
Who could benefit: Volantis and Coherent COHR.

WHY IT MATTERS
We rate it Stage 1, Paper, readiness 32 out of 100. The catch: Nvidia networking SVP: 'If you can use copper, use copper' — optics only where distance forces it. Our judgment: watch. Not financial advice.
