FrontierRadar explainer: Silicon photonics reaches high-volume factories
Issue: 2026-10-30 | Stage 3 · Factories & orders | Readiness 84/100 | Voice: Piper en_US-lessac-medium (local)

HOOK
Light-based chips are now in mass production.

WHAT IT IS
STMicroelectronics' PIC100 silicon-photonics process is at volume.

ANALOGY
Think of the printing press replacing monks copying books by hand. Same words, but suddenly thousands of copies, cheaply.

HOW IT WORKS
First, ST makes light-based circuits on its 300 millimetre wafer lines. Second, they go into 800G and 1.6T optical links for hyperscaler data centres.

BEFORE
Before, photonics was mostly research.

AFTER
Now ST is at volume, with plans to more than quadruple capacity by 2027.

WHY IT MATTERS
This is paper to profit. STMicro, ticker STM, plus Coherent and Lumentum are the exposure. Our judgment: build position. Not financial advice.
