HOOK
Google DeepMind's Genie 4 builds interactive worlds that talk back.

WHAT IT IS
It is a world model that generates real-time simulations with sound and dialogue.

ANALOGY
Think of a theatre stage that builds its scenery as the actors walk, and adds the crowd noise and the other characters' lines on cue.

HOW IT WORKS
First, the model takes your actions as input. Second, it generates the next moment of the world, picture and sound, in real time.

BEFORE
Before, training robots and agents needed real environments or hand-built simulators.

AFTER
Now Genie 4 is a research preview for trusted testers.

WHY IT MATTERS
World models could become training grounds for robots. Preview only. Alphabet is the exposure. Our judgment: watch. Not financial advice.
