HOOK
A tiny light switch that blinks billions of times a second to send messages between computers.

WHAT IT IS
A light switch sending 200 billion bits per second per colour.

WHAT IT DOES
A tiny silicon 'slow-light' modulator flips light on and off fast enough to carry 200 Gb/s on a single colour of laser. Think of it like this: A shutter so fast it could blink a whole HD movie across in a fraction of a second.

BEFORE
Next-gen per-lane speed is a key spec for data-centre optics.

AFTER
Who could benefit: Marvell MRVL and Broadcom AVGO.
